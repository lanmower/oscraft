# Hero abilities

Parity target: reference ruleset at SteamDatabase tracking repo `282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b`. Every ability number of the
heroes below comes from that commit's `source/scripts/npc/heroes/hero_<hero>.txt` (`AbilityDefinitions`:
cooldown, mana, cast range, cast point, channel time, `AbilityValues` by level, scepter, shard and talent deltas) and the generic
talents from `npc/npc_abilities.txt`. The heroes' ability values are not in `npc_abilities.txt` at this commit; they live in the
per-hero files.

`scenarios/moba/tools/gen-abilities.mjs` reads those files and writes `scenarios/moba/abilities_ruleset.osd` and `units_summons.osd`;
`scenarios/moba/tools/ability-recipes.mjs` says which engine effect carries which reference key. A key a recipe does not name is listed in the
generated file as `# <ability> unmodelled reference keys`, and a talent with no effect as `# <hero>: talents with no modelled effect`.
Run `node scenarios/moba/tools/gen-abilities.mjs` to regenerate. Scale: 1 reference unit = 15.625 milli, 16 ticks a second.

## Heroes done

axe, crystal_maiden, sven, juggernaut, zuus (Zeus), sniper, lion (their full kits), phantom_lancer (all four 6.88g abilities; Phantom Rush is a stub), dazzle, antimage, lina, lich, tidehunter, slardar, vengefulspirit and omniknight (every 6.88g hero ability, values from `ability.ini` and `war3map.j` of the 6.88g map; their recipes carry a `v688` spec, so no Dota 2 key is read): the abilities, the
generic talents (`<hero>_talents`) and the unique talents wherever the key they change is modelled. Slots: basics, then the
ultimate, then the talent modifiers. The other 111 of the 127 roster heroes in `units_heroes.osd` have no abilities yet.
Not done: PL's Phantom Rush charge (the ability has no effect), stubs with no effect for a mechanic the effect kinds lack: DAZ's Weave, VS's Nether Swap.
Talents, shard and scepter modifiers are Dota 2 constructs that 6.88g does not have: the generic talent slot (`<hero>_talents`) is kept, the recipes of these eight heroes carry no talent, shard or scepter modifier.

## Mechanics added to the engine

Effect kinds `dot`, `status`, `timed_stat`, `aoe`, `dispel`, `chain`, `zone`, `illusion`, `mana`, `if_kill`, `attack` and typed
`damage` (physical, magical, pure) and `heal` (whole hit points, percent of maximum). The kind names are the `effect_kinds` list of `scenarios/moba/0_names.osd`, and their parameter checks are in `rust/crates/data/src/format.rs`. Around them:

- Rank scaling of any effect parameter, cooldown, mana, cast range, cast point (`ability_scaled` effect code 251), proc chance and period; scepter, shard and eight talent
  modifiers per ability (`AXTN`); talents picked at levels 10, 15, 20, 25 (`choose_talent` in `scenarios/moba/talents.lua`); skill gates 1, 3, 5, 7 and 6, 11, 16 (an ultimate gates at its ability data: 6.88g reqLevel 6, levelSkip 5).
- `aoe` `p3` target filter bits: low 12 bits required unit filter, bits 16 to 23 target cap, bit 12 melee targets only, bit 13 ranged targets only (weapon reach of 3125 milli or more, 200 units: 6.88g heroes are 128 to 150 melee and 235 or more otherwise, unit.ini rangeN1), bit 14 targets under a fifth of their maximum life, bit 30 attributed units only. `shield_bonus` `p2` above 0 locks an attributed target out of the bonus for `p2` ticks from its last grant (Pipe of Insight, 800 ticks).
- Cast point (the caster is held, a stun ends it), cast range with walking into range, charges, passive stat abilities, auras,
  procs on attack and when attacked (by chance or every Nth hit), channels that pulse (random ring) and end by stun, silence or
  hex rather than damage (`channel_damage_interrupt=0` in `attr_rules.txt`).
- Summon groups (`ward`, the timed spawn): p0 unit, p1 lifetime in ticks, p2 how many (a square grid around the point), p3 the grid spacing in milli with bit 30 choosing the unit `p0 + rank - 1`; a no-target ability places them at the caster. Expiry is the unit's lifetime stamp, hashed and saved. Lycan's Summon Wolves (`units_688g_summons.osd`) and Shadow Shaman's Mass Serpent Ward use it; the gate checks `slot-summon-group` and `slot-displace-vacuum` run the wolves and Dark Seer's Vacuum pull.
- Per-enemy stacks and attacker identity (`scenarios/moba/stack_spells.lua`): the `on_hit` hook names attacker and victim and `on_cast` the caster and its target, so a spec keeps stacks and timed debuffs on the victim as counters (`<key>_amount`, `<key>_due`, plus a source counter), expires them in `on_tick` and rebuilds them in `on_state_replaced`. Sticky Napalm (stacks to 10, 8 s, damage on Batrider's hits, war3map.j:61660-61735) and Untouchable (-40/-70/-100/-130 percent attack speed for 4 s on an attacker or caster, war3map.j:48543-48562; the B39C condition is UNVERIFIED) use it; the gate checks `slot-napalm-stacks` and `slot-untouchable-attacker` run them.
- Walls (`wall`, effect kind 38): p0 length in milli-units, p1 lifetime in ticks, p2 thickness in cells, p3 mode (0 a line from two cells ahead of the caster toward the cast point, 1 a line centred on the cast point across the caster's direction). Each cell of the line becomes a temporary blocker (`add_blocker`) that expires with the lifetime. Earthshaker's Fissure (24 segments of 60 map units, 8 s, war3map.j:48451-48471) is the first user; the two-cell start offset is UNVERIFIED.
- Percent auras: an `aoe` (filter 5, allies) over a `timed_stat` with p3 = 1 refreshed every period (`aura=True`) gives allies a percent of their own damage or armour while they stand in range (and a flat percent stat otherwise). Neutral Pack Leader and War Drums damage (15 percent, 900 map units, ability.ini:28899 and 123589) use it; the gate check `slot-percent-aura` runs it.
- Forced displacement (`displace`, effect kind 34): p0 distance in milli-units (0 on a pull: all the way), p1 mode (0 away from the cast point, 1 toward the cast point, 2 toward the caster, 3 away from the caster, 4 the caster toward the cast point), p2 ticks the movement takes (0: at once), p3 area radius around the cast point (0: the cast target; inside an `aoe` the point is the aoe's centre and p3 stays 0). A pull never passes its origin. Roots and stuns do not stop it, spell immunity does for enemies, the victim's channel breaks and its order clears, and the line ends at the first cell that is not walkable. State: `disp_left`, `disp_vx`, `disp_vy` per unit, hashed and saved (state format 51). `Sim::displaced(i)` reads it. Used by DS's Vacuum and Magnus's Reverse Polarity.
- Hero duration, chain growth, strike lifesteal: `stun`/`slow`/`silence`/`root` p2 gives heroes their own tick count (Time Lock 1 s, 2 s on heroes); `chain` p3 bits 24 to 30 grow the damage per jump (Wrath of Nature 7 percent); `attack` p1 adds lifesteal to that one strike (Moment of Courage 55 to 85 percent). `trigger_spells_hero.lua` holds Backtrack (heal back a hit by chance), Press the Attack's dispel, Overwhelming Odds (damage by units and heroes counted, move speed), Duel (both fighters forced, winner's permanent damage), Pulse Nova (toggle, mana per second, scepter damage), Time Walk's path slow and Call Down's scepter missile damage; `furion_treant` is the Force of Nature ward group, Teleportation targets a point and Sprout a unit or point.
- Scaled damage (`damage_by`, effect kind 35): p0 base whole hit points, p1 what scales it (1 caster strength, 2 caster agility, 3 caster intelligence, 4 target current life, 5 target maximum life, 6 target missing life, 7 target mana, 8 caster intelligence minus target intelligence, at least 0, 9 distance from caster to target: p3 hundredths of a hit point per 1000 milli), p2 damage type (1 physical, 2 magical, 3 pure), p3 percent of the scaling value added to p0. Attribute points are the unit counters `attr_str`, `attr_agi`, `attr_int` that `heroes.lua` publishes from the hero's level, base, gain, items and bonuses. Used by Morphling's Adaptive Strike, Necrophos's Reaper's Scythe and Outworld Devourer's Sanity's Eclipse.
- Delayed hits: a `zone` (period 1, life 1, delay n) runs a helper ability n+1 ticks after the cast; `zone` p3 bit 29 pins it to the cast target, so the helper (target `unit`) hits that unit wherever it then stands (Laguna Blade 4 ticks, Finger of Death 4 ticks); without the pin the helper runs at the cast point (Impale's line, 8 ticks). `aoe` p3 bits 24 to 28 taper a line: its half-width falls linearly to n/32 of p0 at the line's end (Dragon Slave 275 to 200 is 23). `mana` mode 3 sacrifices a friendly non-hero target and gives the caster p0 percent of its current hit points as mana (Dark Ritual).
- Path effects on a blink (`move`): p0 cap in milli-units, p1 path half-width in milli-units (0: none), p2 bit 0 carries enemy heroes on the path to the landing point (kept at their offset across the line, spell immunity resists), bit 1 deals p3 magical damage to every enemy non-structure on the path. Used by Magnus's Skewer (carry, then an `aoe` at the landing point for damage and slow) and Earth Spirit's Rolling Boulder (damage).
- Item forms (`item_form`, effect kind 36, only from an item use): p0 the item the used slot becomes (`item="name"` resolves it), p1 mode (0 swap into p0 keeping the slot and its cooldown, 1 spend one charge and swap only when none is left), p2 charges of the new form (0: that item's own charge count, at least 1), p3 ticks the new form cannot be dropped or sold (0 none; `game.item_lock_left`). Toggles, use-count chains and empty-charge copies are data: Armlet, Radiance, Power Treads, Basilius, Black King Bar, Diffusal Blade.
- Execute (`execute`, effect kind 37): p0 the life at or under which the cast target dies outright, p1 the damage otherwise, p2 its type, p3 bit 1 resets the casting ability's own cooldown when the kill is a hero. Used by Axe's Culling Blade (war3map.j:61225-61231).
- Distance falloff on `displace`: p1 plus 8 scales the distance by 1 - d/p3 (d from the origin, p3 the falloff radius in milli-units, at least 16 milli), singly or inside an `aoe`. Used by Drow Ranger's Gust (war3map.j:90436) and Batrider's Flamebreak.
- Mana burn damage: a `mana` effect in mode 1 with p2 above 0 also deals p2 percent of the mana actually burned as typed damage p3 (Anti-Mage's Mana Break, war3map.j:23036).
- Lua specs of abilities and passives whose 6.88g effect is a war3map.j trigger live in `scenarios/moba/trigger_spells.lua`, `trigger_spells_a_b.lua` and `trigger_spells_c_d.lua` (heroes by first letter; shared helpers in `spell_kit.lua`; each module has its own 2,500 step hook budget): on the cast, on a hit (all damage), on a death, per tick; a timed result is a unit counter, from which `on_state_replaced` rebuilds the module list. A timed miss chance is the counter `miss_q16` read by `unit_rules.lua`, and damage taken is the damage reduction stat (code 30, negative amplifies).
- Area filters beyond team and kind: `aoe` p3 bit 12 keeps only units owned by the caster, bit 13 only units that are not heroes. Stat 31 (`attack_bonus`) is a percent bonus on the damage of the next attack, spent by it; carried by a `timed_stat`.
- Illusions (`illusion`), dispels (`dispel`), summons (`ward` with an aura on the placed unit), blinks (`move`).
- Statuses: taunt, hex, disarm, spell immunity, invulnerable, break, mute, physical immunity, death prevention, blind (the `status` effect's p2 is the miss percent, 0 meaning 100: TNK's Laser) and revealed (detected by every player: SLA's Amplify Damage, SNI's Assassinate, Zeus's bolt and Thundergod's Wrath; pierces spell immunity).
- Attack modifiers from stats: evasion, critical strike, cleave (a cone), lifesteal, bash, attack speed, range, magic resistance,
  spell amplification, cooldown reduction, mana and health regeneration, attributes.
- Armour, magic resistance and spell amplification apply to typed spell damage; hero magic resistance 25 percent. A damage effect may also carry an attack type (FORMAT.md, spell damage types): the hit then takes the attack-type factor against the target's armour type instead of the base magic resistance, which is how a hero takes the 6.88g script's NORMAL (0.75), HERO (1.0) and engine-spell (0.75) damage.

## What differs from reference and why

- Charges exist (SNI's Shrapnel: 3 charges, one restored per 55 s, 6.88g A064). Talents, shard and scepter modifiers are Dota 2 constructs that 6.88g does not have; Zeus and SNI carry only the generic talent slot.
- Facets are deprecated in the dump and not modelled. Shard and scepter values are modelled only where a recipe names the key;
  the shard and scepter abilities themselves (Dota 2 shard and scepter abilities that 6.88g does not have) are not.
- Projectile speeds of LIO's Impale and travel times of instant-effect bolts are not modelled (Impale lands along its line at once).
- Freezing Field fires 80 explosions of 1.25 times the damage instead of 100 (a tick is 62.5 ms); Mana Drain pulses
  every 4 ticks so every step is a whole number.
- JUG (6.88g, ability.ini and war3map.j cited in the generated comments): the healing ward does not follow a unit target and heals once per second instead of every 0.1 s; Blade Fury pulses every 4 ticks (19 pulses, first one a period after the cast, damage rounded to whole numbers); Omnislash slashes every 6 ticks for the midpoint 213 of 200 to 225 physical and has no instant kill of non-hero units. SVE: God's Strength has no transformation. Illusion-specific rules beyond damage dealt and taken are not modelled.
- AXE and CM take every number from 6.88g (ability.ini and war3map.j, cited in the generated file's comments); talents, shard and
  scepter modifiers are Dota 2 constructs 6.88g does not have, so only the generic talents remain. CM: Frostbite lasts
  its hero duration on every target (10 s on non-heroes in 6.88g). Zeus: Static
  Field is a trigger spell (trigger_spells.lua), the bolt's ground cast is `GROUND_RETARGET`; the bolt's true sight is a revealed status on the unit struck (a guess). SNI: Headshot's attack speed slow. LIO: the Impale travel speed. PL: Phantom Rush's charge and agility, Doppelganger's 1 s vanish, random spots and
  regrouping, Juxtapose's illusion cap and illusion-born illusions.
- Missing for the 6.88g heroes of the generated ruleset: waves (Wave of Terror, Ravage) land at once with a constant width; DAZ: Poison Touch's staged slow, delayed damage and stun, Shadow Wave's bounce beyond two units, Weave's accumulating armor; LIN: Dragon Slave's missile travel time; LIC: Chain Frost's slow and random target; TID: Kraken Shell's flat block, Ravage's knock-up; SLA: Sprint's phasing, Amplify Damage's true sight; VS: Command Aura's reverted aura on death, Nether Swap's exchange; OMN: Repel's dispel on cast.
- Trigger abilities of the heroes a to d (each spec cites its war3map.j lines): Battle Hunger ends when its target or its killer dies and gives Axe 12 percent movement per afflicted enemy (cap 6 is a GUESS); Bloodrage heals the enraged killer and its caster when it dies (GUESS who heals); Nightmare sleeps by stun, pulses 20 pure a second, ignores hits by its own caster (GUESS) and gives its attacker the same sleep; Glimpse samples hero positions each second and returns the target to the oldest of five; Weave takes the hero group once at the cast and floors the accumulated armor; Aphotic Shield reads the shield each tick and releases its full amount within 700 when it breaks or ends; Borrowed Time is death prevention plus a heal of twice the damage dealt, automatic under 400 life with the ability ready; Stampede touches heroes only (GUESS: the 2500 step hook budget); Devour kills at once and pays 25 per rank (the digestion delay is not modelled); Persuasion caps converted units at the rank and kills the oldest; Pit of Malice hits each unit once for 7 s; Flux hurts and slows only while its target is alone; Firefly burns every trail point; Rupture is pure damage per distance moved (GUESS type); Poison Touch slows in stages, stuns at rank 4 and hurts from the third second; Shadow Wave bounces to the most wounded allies within 500; Wind Walk arms a 30 x rank backstab on the first hit; Spiderlings spawn where a marked unit dies; Fiend's Grip reveals its target and drains 5 percent of its mana a second; Spark Wraith strikes the first enemy near it; Chilling Touch charges allied attacks; Flaming Lasso leashes its target (GUESS); Wall of Replica hits each enemy hero crossing it once and leaves an image for Dark Seer; Death Prophet's Exorcism places ghosts that heal her; Dark Rift is a cast point by rank that takes allied heroes along; Chemical Rage sets the base attack time; Tempest Double copies skills and items through spawn_image; Wild Axes sweep two curved paths; Shuriken Toss bounces to Tracked heroes; Kinetic Field is a ring of blockers; Primal Split removes the Brewmaster for three elementals; scepter: Rupture charges, Fiend's Grip damage, Doom break; the scepter widens Viscous Goo and gives Phantasm an extra illusion half the time.
- Spell immunity pierce comes from the ability's own flag (`SpellImmunityType`); dispel strength is a mask, not reference types.
- Gate checks: the `*_checks.rs` files in `rust/gate/src`. `GATE_ONLY=<text> cargo run -p oscraft-gate` runs only the checks whose names contain the text.
