# How a match plays

The `scenarios/moba` pack is a match: the engine has no scenario-specific code, the rules are Lua modules in that directory, and every number here comes from its `.osd` files. The full tables are in [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md), generated from the same files.

**Provenance.** A figure is UNVERIFIED unless its `.osd` comment cites a source. Cited figures: every `npc_units.txt` stat of lane creeps, towers, barracks, the ancient and neutral types, the 6.88g stats of Roshan, the wards and the courier, and the wave, siege, camp, Roshan and rune timers listed under Units and structures: reference parity; the cost of the blink item. The abilities of eight heroes (axe, crystal_maiden, sven, juggernaut, zuus, sniper, lion, omniknight) are reference, see [ABILITIES.md](ABILITIES.md). Everything else (item costs and stats, timers, positions, the abilities of the other heroes) is a placeholder. Where this page marks a figure UNVERIFIED, expect it to change.

Time: the sim runs 16 ticks per second. Distances are milli-units; one map cell is 1000 (64 reference units).

## Map

The map is the reference ruleset map (patch 7.41 data) at its real GridNav size, generated by `scenarios/moba/tools/gen-reference-map.mjs` into `map.osd`. The neutral camps of `neutral.osd` are the 6.88g camps, generated from the 6.88g map script by `scenarios/moba/tools/gen-neutral-688g.mjs` (`--script <war3map.j> [--check]`); the lines after its `# Roshan` marker are kept by hand. Do not hand-edit the camp lines; edit the script and rerun it.

**Size.** The world spans x and y from -10464 to 10400 reference units (20864 wide). GridNav cells are 64 units, so the grid is 327 x 327 = 106,929 cells (`grid(327, 327, cell_milli=1000)`). The source marks 47,323 cells untraversable; the generator also blocks 2,303 cells for the 2,475 trees (trees are static here, there is no tree cutting) and re-opens the footprints of buildings (units, not terrain, are the obstacles). That leaves 57,303 walkable cells, 57,215 of them connected to the Radiant fountain (the other 88 are pockets). **Scale:** 1 reference unit = 15.625 milli, so one 64-unit cell is one 1000-milli sim cell and reference speeds, ranges and radii keep their ratio to the map. Sim x grows east and sim y grows south on screen (sim y = -reference y), so Radiant (team 0) is bottom-left and Dire (team 1) top-right, as in the game.

**Layout (from the dump).** Fountains at (-7456, -6938) Radiant and (7408, 6848) Dire; ancients (`base`) at (-5920, -5352) and (5528, 5000). Per side: tier 1, 2 and 3 towers on each of the top, middle and bottom lanes and two tier 4 towers beside the ancient (11 per side, 22 in all); three melee and three ranged barracks, one pair per lane; 7 filler buildings as props only. Four shops (one at each fountain, two on the sides at (-5080, 1948) and (4886, -1208)), two outposts at (-4096, -448) and (3392, -448), two power rune spots at (-1640, 1112) and (1180, -1216), two bounty rune spots at (-996, 4431) and (595, -4660), two Roshan pits at (2831, -2740) and (-3194, 2395), two Tormentor spawns, two lotus pools, two twin gates, two xp fountains, four landmarks and ten lanterns (props only). 28 neutral camps (14 per side) at the dump's spawner positions. Terrain height comes from the dump's elevation grid: level 0 river and low ground, 1 field and lanes, 2 base high ground, 3 and 4 cliffs and rims (clamped), `height_step(2000)`. The diagonal river, the high-ground ramps and the lane shapes are the data's own, not drawn by hand.

**Not sourced or approximated (UNVERIFIED).** Liquipedia and Fandom are Cloudflare-blocked and the reference developer wiki holds no coordinates, so the GitHub dump is the only map source. Approximated: the 128-unit height step (2000 milli per level) and the clamp at level 4; the ten hero start cells (the dump has no start entity: five open cells on a 520-unit ring around each fountain, facing the ancient) and the tender and courier cells beside them; the mapping of the dump's neutralType 0..3 to small, medium, large and ancient camps (the counts 6, 14, 6 and 2 fit, nothing confirms it) and every camp composition; camp units spread on a 110-unit ring around the spawner point; trees as permanent blocks. Not modelled: Roshan sits at the first pit only (the second pit and the day/night swap are a prop marker); power runes, Tormentors, lotus pools, twin gates and xp fountains are markers without gameplay; outposts are neutral structures without capture; the secret shops of older patches are absent from the dump.

**Sources.** the leamare map-coordinates dump (github.com/leamare) default branch, read on 2026-10-18: `data/worlddata.json`, `data/gridnavdata.json`, `data/elevationdata.json`, `data/mapdata.json`, `mapdata.json`, `data/lanedata.json` (a dump of the game's entities and GridNav). The reference developer wiki page `Dota_2_Workshop_Tools/Level_Design/reference/Navigation_Mesh` for the 64-unit GridNav cell. `SteamDatabase tracking repo` @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b for unit stats (see the sections below).

## Sides and players

Ten players, two teams of five: the `start(...)` lines of `map.osd` give each player a start point and a team, and the even players are on team 0, the odd on team 1. A seat's hero spawns at its start point when the lobby picks it (`seat_pick` in `heroes.lua`). The lobby pool holds the ten hero types, five per side.

## Lanes and creeps

Six `lane_spawner` units (three per side, air layer, immune, no weapon) stand at each side's melee barracks and cast the lane waves (`waves.osd`). Every 30 s (480 ticks, the reference interval) each lane gets three melee creeps and one ranged creep. Siege creeps start at 5:00 (4800 ticks) and then come every 5:00, one per lane, two per lane from 30:00 and three from 60:00 (reference wiki reference patches 7.06 and 7.41): each siege ability starts on cooldown (`initial_cooldown`) and its ready trigger casts it. Creeps march on the route of their lane when the map has one, otherwise on the nearest enemy building, and fight what they meet. Idle units with a weapon (creeps, towers, bases) strike the nearest enemy in reach. A wave creep that spawns in the first 329 s of game time (6.88g's 420 s map clock less the 91 s countdown: GUESS, the lobby delay before it is taken as 0) gets 25 percent more speed (Scourge top, Sentinel bottom) or 25 percent less (Scourge bottom, Sentinel top) for 15 s, through `game.cmd_timed_stat` (6.88g H3E and H4E); mid is untouched.

Super and mega creeps: `lanes.lua` chooses each wave unit's kind (its base, super or mega kind, from `creep`, `ranged` and `siege` in its table). A wave unit spawns as its super kind once the enemy tower that mirrors its spawn point is dead, and as its mega kind once every enemy tower is dead. Super and mega creeps have identical stats in the dump (700 hp melee, 475 hp ranged), so mega differs only by the rule that spawns it.

Unit scale: 1 reference unit = 15.625 milli; speed is milli per tick; weapon period = AttackRate x 100 / BaseAttackSpeed x 16 ticks; health_regen is milli-hp per tick (reference hp/s x 62.5). Stats are read from `npc_units.txt` @282fad29 (health, armor, damage midpoint, rate, range, speed, vision, regen, gold midpoint, xp).

Creeps (cited): melee 550 hp, ranged 300 hp, siege 935 hp, plus super and mega variants (`creep_super`, `creep_mega`, ...). Still different from reference: the time-based creep stat and bounty growth (every 7:30; the growth numbers are not in the dump and Liquipedia and Fandom are blocked), extra melee and ranged creeps late in the game, the flagbearer, magic resistance (siege 80, flagbearer 40), which barracks upgrades the siege creep (melee is assumed, UNVERIFIED), and the lane creeps' aggro rules beyond `aggro.rs`. Hull radii are the dump's RingRadius scaled to milli.

## Towers and bases

Units defined from the dump: `tower` (T1: 1800 hp, armor 12, damage 90, range 700 reference units), `tower_t2` and `tower_t3` (2500 hp, armor 16, damage 172), `tower_t4` (2600 hp, armor 21), `barracks_melee` (2200 hp, armor 15, regen 5), `barracks_ranged` (1300 hp, armor 9), `base` (the ancient: 4500 hp, armor 23, regen 12) and `outpost` (450 hp). Tower gold and barracks gold are paid by code in reference and are not in the dump: not modelled. The map places them at the dump's real positions: the tier is in the unit name (`tower` is tier 1), two `tower_t4` stand beside each ancient, and the barracks pairs sit at their lane ends.

The base carries the `building` filter. When the last living building of a side dies, the rival wins (`win.lua`, `on_check_win`).

## Heroes

Roster: every enabled reference hero (127, from `npc_heroes` and `heroes/hero_*.txt` @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b) is a unit in `scenarios/moba/units_heroes.osd`, generated by `node scenarios/moba/tools/gen-heroes.mjs`, which also writes the hero rule block of `scenarios/moba/attr_rules.txt` (primary attribute, base attributes and per-level gains, mana, mana and health regeneration, armor, magic resistance, base attack time, damage midpoint). Sixteen of them hold their reference abilities (axe, crystal_maiden, sven, juggernaut, zuus, sniper, lion, omniknight, phantom_lancer, dazzle, lina, lich, tidehunter, slardar, antimage, vengefulspirit), generated from reference hero files by `node scenarios/moba/tools/gen-abilities.mjs` into `abilities_ruleset.osd` (what is and is not modelled: [ABILITIES.md](ABILITIES.md)); the rest have none yet (`abilities=[]`). The ten placeholder heroes (`hero`, `ironhide`, `swiftbow`, `stormcaller`, `bulwark`, `shadeblade`, `emberlord`, `gale_archer`, `frostmage`, `tidecaller`, `thornwarden`) keep their invented kits but carry the reference stats of a stand-in hero (`STANDINS` in the script: `dragon_knight`, `sven`, `drow_ranger`, `zuus`, `axe`, `riki`, `lina`, `windrunner`, `crystal_maiden`, `kunkka`, `treant`) until a real kit replaces each one.

Matched to reference (sourced): health 120 base, movement speed, attack range, vision (daytime), base armor, damage min/max midpoint, base attack time (attack period = BAT x 16 ticks / (1 + agility/100)), base attributes and gains, primary attribute, health regeneration, mana 75 base and mana regeneration. Attribute coefficients are 6.88g misc.ini values (StrHitPointBonus 19, StrRegenBonus 0.03, IntManaBonus 13, IntRegenBonus 0.04, AgiDefenseBonus 0.14 with AgiDefenseBase -1.0, AgiAttackSpeedBonus 0.01); 1 damage per primary point and 0.7 per point of every attribute for a universal hero are UNVERIFIED (not in the 6.88g data). Hero level 1 is zero levels gained; level-up recomputes the stats.

Still UNVERIFIED or not modelled for heroes: hull radius 375 milli (24 reference units, an engine constant not in the dump), turn rate, backswing, magic resistance (stored, `hero_magic_resist_pct`, not yet applied by the damage code), the facets and the talent tree, and the reference xp curve past what `xp.rs` carries.

Skills: a hero spends one skill point per character level. Ordinary abilities learn to rank 4 at levels 1, 3, 5 and 7 (the gate is data); the ultimate learns to rank 3 at levels 6, 12 and 18; talents come at levels 10, 15, 20 and 25, one of two per tier. The xp curve reaches level 18 (cumulative xp 100, 250, 500, ... 15200; UNVERIFIED). Mechanics covered by the roster: nuke, projectile, area damage, blink, heal over time, aura, passive stat, summon, channel, and the status effects stun, slow, silence, root and invisibility. Passives are stat abilities with cooldown 100000 cast once at spawn by a `spawned` behaviour.

## Respawn and buyback

Sources: patch notes from the reference wiki datafeed (7.24 to 7.40) and `npc_heroes`/`npc_units` @282fad29. A dead hero respawns after `floor(3.8 * level + 5)` seconds with no cap (6.88g war3map.j F_V 14613); Bloodstone and Reaper's Scythe respawn terms are not modelled. Buyback costs `50 * (1 + clamp(floor((100 + 1.5 * level^2 + 15 * minutes) / 50), 0, 59))` gold (q2 8036-8047), has a 420 s cooldown (w2 8139), adds a quarter of the remaining respawn timer to the next death and cuts bounties to 0.4 until the timer would have ended (B5 10830-10836, CU 3684). Hero last-hit gold is `125 + 8 * level` (7.31 notes) and each assisting hero gets `15 + (50 + 0.037 * victim net worth) / assisting heroes` (7.40 notes). Armor reduces physical damage by `0.06a / (1 + 0.06|a|)` with no floor; heroes take ability damage reduced by their magic resistance (25 base, `hero_base`). Movement speed is held to 100..550 (7.38 notes; one reference speed point is 64 in Q16.16). The xp table (230, 600, 1080 ... 45595 for levels 2 to 30) is an engine constant recalled, UNVERIFIED past level 8. Still not reference: turn rate, attack point and backswing, projectile speed, evasion, per-unit night vision, fountain regeneration, death gold loss, streak bounties, tower and Roshan magic resistance.

## Items and shops

Source: `items.txt` and `neutral_items.txt` of SteamDatabase tracking repo @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b, turned into `scenarios/moba/items.osd` by `scenarios/moba/tools/gen-items.mjs` (do not edit the `.osd` by hand). 313 items: every non-obsolete item of the file, with reference cost (the full price), the first requirement set of its recipe as components (up to six; the recipe scroll is not an item, its price is the cost less the components), consumable and stack data (`ItemInitialCharges`, `ItemStackable`), shop stock (`ItemStockMax`, `ItemStockInitial`, `ItemStockTime`, `ItemInitialStockTime`) and the neutral tiers, with the neutral items and enhancement levels of `neutral_items.txt`. Every stat key the generator knows (`bonus_*` and the unprefixed forms of the neutral items) becomes an item stat; scale is 15.625 milli per reference unit and 16 ticks per second.

6.88g layer (6.88g wins over the reference dump): `scenarios/moba/items_688g.tsv` holds one row per item with the 6.88g item it is matched to and the method (exact_name, exact_composition; anything weaker is a candidate and is not applied), and `scenarios/moba/tools/apply-items-688g.mjs` writes its cost, recipe components, stat bonuses and active cooldown onto `items.osd` (`--check` fails when the file drifts from the table; it also checks that a recipe never costs less than its components, which store.lua would pay out). Costs are the item.ini `goldcost`; stat bonuses are read from the item tooltip, and a stat the tooltip is silent on keeps its value (UNVERIFIED). Rows with status `floor` or `candidate_floor` have no 6.88g price: their cost is raised to the price of their components. Shops: the 6.88g shop tables are the only selling rule: an item sells only at the shops that list it, and an item no shop lists is not sold. `scenarios/moba/shops_688g.tsv` lists, per shop prop of `map.osd`, the 6.88g item codes the shop sells: the recipe-proxy route (`sold_by`), the shop unit's `Sellunits` proxies resolved through the `ZXR` proxy table of `war3map.j`, and the script's own stock (`AddUnitToStock`: Observer Wards at `e025` and `u00Q` by `RZX` lines 36672-36673, the Tome of Knowledge at both Trophy Dealers by `JAE` lines 26257-26258). `shops_extra_688g.tsv` places the tagged items that no 6.88g purchase table lists, one row per item with its shops and a source label: `6.88g` (derived from the script or unit data), `web` (a public reference) or `GUESS` (placement by the item's `items.osd` tags: home at Arcane Sanctum, side at Goblin Merchant, secret at Leragas The Vile). `tools/gen-shops-688g.mjs` merges both into `shops_688g.lua` and refuses a tagged buyable item no shop sells. `shops_unadded_688g.tsv` declares the listed 6.88g codes that have no oscraft item (recipe scrolls and items not yet added). `tools/check-shops-688g.mjs` (ci.yml) fails when these files, `items.osd` and the generated Lua disagree.

6.88g item codes that are not an item of their own are listed with their rule in `scenarios/moba/items_688g_na.tsv`: `not_applicable` rows are hero Aghanim's Scepter copies, courier editions, unreachable placeholders and opt-in ward modes; every toggle, melee/ranged form, charge and cooldown state, and courier edition 6.88g keeps as its own item code is an item of its own (`item_form` use effect, `spell_block`/`true_strike` stats, `item_forms.lua`: armlet on/off with the 40 hp/s drain, Radiance, Monkey King Bar, Power Treads, Basilius and Aquila forms, the Black King Bar chain 10 to 5 with the per-hero use counter, Diffusal and Janggo empty copies and the Janggo recharge, Heart, Tranquil Boots and Linken's Sphere copies, the Alchemist transferable scepter, Divine Rapier's undroppable enemy copy, courier gem and armlet); the per-hero Aghanim copies stay one `ultimate_scepter` (decision in each row). Tome of Knowledge (shop-only at the two Trophy Dealers, `tome.lua`: 700 xp plus 135 for each tome the team already used) and the Disabled Kelen's Dagger state (a Blink cast refused within 3 s of taking a hit, `spells.lua`) are implemented.

## Neutrals

Thirty-eight reference neutral types are defined as `neutral_<name>` units with dump stats (health, regen, armor, damage, attack time, range, speed, vision, bounty). Camp compositions are UNVERIFIED: small = kobold taskmaster, tunneler, kobold; medium = alpha wolf and two giant wolves; large = centaur khan and two outrunners; ancient = granite golem and two rock golems. Neutral abilities are not modelled. The map holds 28 camps of three creeps (player -1) at the dump's spawner points, 14 per side, answering attacks through the shared `attacked` behaviour. A camp unit that strays more than 10938 milli (700 reference units, UNVERIFIED) from home walks back.

The first regular camp set appears at 0:30 (`camp_first_spawn_s=30` in `attr_rules.txt`; 6.88g map script PXE); Roshan and the bounty runes are present from the start. The 14 camps of the 6.88g script (`camps_688g.lua`, generated by `tools/gen-neutral-688g.mjs`) run the camp pass at 0:30 and on every whole minute of the game clock after: a camp whose check rect holds no living unit rolls one of its options (never the option it rolled last) and creates all of that option's members together, each 25 to 60 war units from the spawn centre (war3map.j KPE 26823-26845, KBE 26738-26753, KJE 26812-26816, JWE 26627-26632). A camp with a survivor in its rect is not respawned, so pulling a creep out of the rect and waiting for the minute mark stacks a second set, as in the script. The map's placed members are the camp's slots: they respawn as the option's first members (`cmd_respawn_as`), further members are spawned and join the group, and slots beyond the option stay dead.

## Roshan

A large neutral boss in the single pit of 6.88g, at the centre of the map script's rect Pn (war units 4208, -2288; milli 228750, 198750). He stands from 0:00. Health 7500, damage 75 at 1.0 s, armor 6, regen 20, speed 270, bounty 275 gold (midpoint of the script's 150 to 400) and 1789 xp (script table UUR 91913-91915), all from 6.88g `n00L`. Every 240 s of game time he gains 500 health and 0.7 armor (armor for the first 20 steps), and each new Roshan starts with the bonus of that moment. He respawns 480 to 660 s after death (script `GetRandomInt(480,660)`). A Roshan outside the pit rect (leash rect centre 221250, 200500 milli, half sides 11250 and 6000) is set back at the pit centre by the second check, 10 s apart. His death pays 200 gold to every living hero of the killing side and drops the `aegis` where he died for any hero to take: the first living hero within the pickup range (1500 milli, the pack takes no pick-up order) with a free slot takes it, and from the third death on a `cheese` lies beside it. A carried aegis is valid for 300 s of the death (a warning to the holder's allies at 240 s), then every carried aegis charge is taken back, and again when the next Roshan appears; an aegis still on the ground is not taken back, as the script leaves it. His slam, bash, 75 percent spell damage reduction and ghost form are not modelled. 6.88g has one pit, no Tormentor, no outposts and no pit swap.

## Runes

Two rune spots at the centres of the map script's river rects (war units -2352, 1648 and 3008, -2832; milli 126250, 137250 and 210000, 207250). At 0:00 both hold a first bounty (100 gold, no xp). Every 120 s after that the uncollected runes vanish and one of the two spots, chosen at random, holds a bounty rune worth 50 + 2 per game minute in gold and 50 + 5 per game minute in xp (script H7V and QIV). A living hero within 1500 milli takes it (the pickup range is UNVERIFIED). The other spot holds a power rune (haste 25 s, double damage 45 s, regeneration 30 s, illusion 75 s, invisibility 45 s), applied at once on pickup by `runes.lua`; invisibility ends at its time or when the hero casts or attacks. A hero that carries a bottle stores the rune in it instead (war3map.j QKV 18568-18627): the emptiest bottle (empty, then 1/3, 2/3, 3/3) becomes the rune bottle (`bottle_haste` and the like), and the rune acts when the bottle is drunk or by itself after 120 s (QDV 18519-18529), the bottle then 3/3 again; a bounty rune pays by the minute it is used (QIV 18458-18482). The bottle (`bottle.lua`) holds three drinks of 110 hit points and 70 mana over 3 s (the 3/3 item's charges); the last drink leaves the empty bottle (`bottle_empty`), the 3/3 bottle is not spent in the own fountain (UBV 20496-20505), and an empty, 1/3 or 2/3 bottle refills in the fountain. A hero's Divine Rapier and Gem of True Sight fall where it dies and lie there for any hero in range (item.ini drop=1; the script drops nothing else). 6.88g has no water runes, shrines, lotus pools or outpost capture.

## Vision at night

Day lasts 240 s and night 240 s (`clock.lua`). A unit kind with a night sight (`night_sight_milli` of its `unit_extra`, UEXT since format 27) sees that far at night: heroes 800 reference units (sniper and bounty hunter 1000, night stalker and slark 1800, from the `nsight` of their `unit.ini` rows), towers 800, barracks 600, ancients and fountains 1800, Roshan 1400, couriers 350. Every other kind sees its day sight divided by the `night_sight_divisor` rule (2, UNVERIFIED: the stock night sight of the remaining kinds is in the map's SLK files, which are not in the data). Creeps sleeping at night (`canSleep`) are not modelled.

## Attack and armour types

A weapon hit is the damage after the armour curve, times the factor of the weapon's attack type against the target's armour type (`scenarios/moba/damage_table.osd`, `damage_type` and `size` on each `unit(...)` line). 6.88g's misc.ini sets the Normal, Pierce, Siege, Magic and Chaos rows (misc.ini:23-31); the Hero row (50 percent against fortified, 5 percent against divine) is the Warcraft III custom-game default the map leaves alone. Every hero has the hero attack and the hero armour; lane creeps carry unarmored armour, towers, barracks, base buildings and the ancients fortified, Roshan and the neutrals their unit.ini type. Effects: creeps hit heroes at 75 percent (normal), 50 percent (pierce) or 75 percent (siege); siege hits fortified at 150 percent; pierce hits unarmored at 150 percent; heroes' own attacks do 50 percent to fortified buildings. Neutrals with no row in the 6.88g data (prowler acolyte and shaman, granite golem, ice shaman, frostbitten golem) keep normal attack against small armour (factor 1). Ability damage does not go through the table (the script's spell damage is the Hero attack type, 1.0 against every armour but fortified and divine; engine-run spells use DamageBonusSpells, hero 0.75).

Armour: a hit is multiplied by the engine's curve, `1 - 0.06 a / (1 + 0.06 a)` for armour `a` above zero and `2 - 0.94^(-a)` below zero (armour -10 takes 1.461 of a hit), 0.06 being the custom-game DefenseArmor the map leaves alone (`unit_rules.lua`). A hero's armour is its base plus 0.14 per agility less 1.0 (misc.ini AgiDefenseBonus, AgiDefenseBase); damage per primary attribute point is 1 (StrAttackBonus, unchanged by the map).

## Attack timing

A weapon attack starts when the unit's cooldown is up and its target is in reach, and its hit lands later: after the kind's attack point (6.88g unit.ini dmgpt of the active weapon, `unit_extra` `attack_point_ms`; hero Anti-Mage 0.3 s, lane melee creep 0.467 s, tower 0.6 s), which attack speed shortens like it shortens the cooldown, plus, for a missile weapon, the flight over the distance at the kind's missile speed (`missile_speed`, game units a second: ranged creep 900, tower 750, Sniper 3000). The cooldown runs from the start, so the rate of attacks is the base attack time over attack speed as before. A hit is lost when its target dies first; a hand-to-hand blow is also lost when its attacker is stunned or disarmed or its target has left reach during the attack point, and a missile always finds a living target. One hit per unit is in flight: a new attack lands the previous one first. Not modelled: the backswing (unit.ini backSw1; nothing in the sim acts in it), the turn rate before an attack (no unit has a facing in the sim), the missile arc and homing flag (a hit is a point of damage), and cancelling an attack by a new order during the attack point (the order does not cancel the hit in flight).

## Denying

An explicit attack order (the attack command on a unit) may target an ally, one of the player's own units or a teammate's, when the sim's `denies` allows it: the ally's life is strictly below its kind's `deny_pct` percent of its maximum. Auto-attack never chooses an ally. The percents are 6.88g's gate (war3map.j P7E 30567-30573, P5E 30561): structures other than the ancients below 10 (towers, barracks, base buildings, objective), every other non-hero unit below 50 (lane creeps, tender, courier, wards, summons), heroes 0, the ancients, fountain, neutrals and Roshan 0. A denied unit pays no gold. A denied lane creep gives enemy heroes 50 percent of its xp; a denied hero gives 0 percent; a teammate's denial counts like the owner's.

## Economy

Gold is the player's bank, the figure the shop spends, as in the 6.88g script. A seat opens on 875 gold (4375 divided by the five players of a side; the script's +175 for a picked hero is not applied: UNVERIFIED) and earns 1 gold every 600 ms, alive or dead (`kill_gold.lua`). A hero kill pays the killer's player 100 + 9 per victim level, plus 60 per step of the victim's kill streak beyond two (from a streak of 3, at most 8 steps), all of it; every allied hero within 1325 units of the victim takes an assist share by the script's table (`ASSIST`), weighted by net worth rank. The first hero death by an enemy pays 200 gold (150 of it reliable); a hero killed by an enemy creep or tower pays its damagers of the last 18 seconds: one damager is credited as the killer, several split the bounty, none leaves the whole enemy team to split it. Every hero death costs the victim min(30 per level, its unreliable gold), a denial included; reliable gold is what kills, assists and structure team gold paid and is lost last. A unit with a bounty pays it to the killing hero alone (`core/bounty.lua`); a creep, tower or neutral killer, a denial and an attackerless death pay no bounty. A tower pays every enemy seat 120 + 40 per tier (half on a denial), a barracks 275 (melee) or 225 (ranged). A hero that dies holding the aegis (`aegis.lua`) is paid and charged nothing for that death. Rune pickups and Roshan add gold as described under Runes. The tender is a data unit with `resource_a`; no moba rule makes it harvest.
