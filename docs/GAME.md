# How a match plays

The `scenarios/moba` pack is a match: the engine has no scenario-specific code, the rules are Lua modules in that directory, and every number here comes from its `.osd` files. The full tables are in [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md), generated from the same files.

**Provenance.** A figure is UNVERIFIED unless its `.osd` comment cites a source. Cited figures: every `npc_units.txt` stat of lane creeps, towers, barracks, the ancient, outposts, neutral types, Roshan, the Tormentor, wards and the courier, and the wave, siege, camp, Roshan, Tormentor and rune timers listed under Units and structures: reference parity; the cost of the blink item. The abilities of eight heroes (axe, crystal_maiden, sven, juggernaut, zuus, sniper, lion, omniknight) are reference, see [ABILITIES.md](ABILITIES.md). Everything else (item costs and stats, timers, positions, the abilities of the other heroes) is a placeholder. Where this page marks a figure UNVERIFIED, expect it to change.

Time: the sim runs 16 ticks per second. Distances are milli-units; one map cell is 1000 (64 reference units).

## Map

The map is the reference ruleset map (patch 7.41 data) at its real GridNav size, generated by `scenarios/moba/tools/gen-reference-map.mjs` into `map.osd` and `neutral.osd`. Do not hand-edit those two files; edit the script and rerun it (`node scenarios/moba/tools/gen-reference-map.mjs`, which downloads the sources).

**Size.** The world spans x and y from -10464 to 10400 reference units (20864 wide). GridNav cells are 64 units, so the grid is 327 x 327 = 106,929 cells (`grid(327, 327, cell_milli=1000)`). The source marks 47,323 cells untraversable; the generator also blocks 2,303 cells for the 2,475 trees (trees are static here, there is no tree cutting) and re-opens the footprints of buildings (units, not terrain, are the obstacles). That leaves 57,303 walkable cells, 57,215 of them connected to the Radiant fountain (the other 88 are pockets). **Scale:** 1 reference unit = 15.625 milli, so one 64-unit cell is one 1000-milli sim cell and reference speeds, ranges and radii keep their ratio to the map. Sim x grows east and sim y grows south on screen (sim y = -reference y), so Radiant (team 0) is bottom-left and Dire (team 1) top-right, as in the game.

**Layout (from the dump).** Fountains at (-7456, -6938) Radiant and (7408, 6848) Dire; ancients (`base`) at (-5920, -5352) and (5528, 5000). Per side: tier 1, 2 and 3 towers on each of the top, middle and bottom lanes and two tier 4 towers beside the ancient (11 per side, 22 in all); three melee and three ranged barracks, one pair per lane; 7 filler buildings as doodads only. Four shops (one at each fountain, two on the sides at (-5080, 1948) and (4886, -1208)), two outposts at (-4096, -448) and (3392, -448), two power rune spots at (-1640, 1112) and (1180, -1216), two bounty rune spots at (-996, 4431) and (595, -4660), two Roshan pits at (2831, -2740) and (-3194, 2395), two Tormentor spawns, two lotus pools, two twin gates, two xp fountains, four landmarks and ten lanterns (doodads only). 28 neutral camps (14 per side) at the dump's spawner positions. Terrain height comes from the dump's elevation grid: level 0 river and low ground, 1 field and lanes, 2 base high ground, 3 and 4 cliffs and rims (clamped), `height_step(2000)`. The diagonal river, the high-ground ramps and the lane shapes are the data's own, not drawn by hand.

**Not sourced or approximated (UNVERIFIED).** Liquipedia and Fandom are Cloudflare-blocked and the reference developer wiki holds no coordinates, so the GitHub dump is the only map source. Approximated: the 128-unit height step (2000 milli per level) and the clamp at level 4; the ten hero start cells (the dump has no start entity: five open cells on a 520-unit ring around each fountain, facing the ancient) and the tender and courier cells beside them; the mapping of the dump's neutralType 0..3 to small, medium, large and ancient camps (the counts 6, 14, 6 and 2 fit, nothing confirms it) and every camp composition; camp units spread on a 110-unit ring around the spawner point; trees as permanent blocks. Not modelled: Roshan sits at the first pit only (the second pit and the day/night swap are a doodad marker); power runes, Tormentors, lotus pools, twin gates and xp fountains are markers without gameplay; outposts are neutral structures without capture; the secret shops of older patches are absent from the dump.

**Sources.** the leamare map-coordinates dump (github.com/leamare) default branch, read on 2026-10-18: `data/worlddata.json`, `data/gridnavdata.json`, `data/elevationdata.json`, `data/mapdata.json`, `mapdata.json`, `data/lanedata.json` (a dump of the game's entities and GridNav). The reference developer wiki page `Dota_2_Workshop_Tools/Level_Design/reference/Navigation_Mesh` for the 64-unit GridNav cell. `SteamDatabase tracking repo` @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b for unit stats (see the sections below).

## Sides and players

Ten players, two teams of five: the `start(...)` lines of `map.osd` give each player a start point and a team, and the even players are on team 0, the odd on team 1. A seat's hero spawns at its start point when the lobby picks it (`seat_pick` in `heroes.lua`). The lobby pool holds the ten hero types, five per side.

## Lanes and creeps

Six `lane_spawner` units (three per side, air layer, immune, no weapon) stand at each side's melee barracks and cast the lane waves (`waves.osd`). Every 30 s (480 ticks, the reference interval) each lane gets three melee creeps and one ranged creep. Siege creeps start at 5:00 (4800 ticks) and then come every 5:00, one per lane, two per lane from 30:00 and three from 60:00 (reference wiki reference patches 7.06 and 7.41): each siege ability starts on cooldown (`initial_cooldown`) and its ready trigger casts it. Creeps march on the route of their lane when the map has one, otherwise on the nearest enemy building, and fight what they meet. Idle units with a weapon (creeps, towers, bases) strike the nearest enemy in reach.

Super and mega creeps: `lanes.lua` chooses each wave unit's kind (its base, super or mega kind, from `creep`, `ranged` and `siege` in its table). A wave unit spawns as its super kind once the enemy tower that mirrors its spawn point is dead, and as its mega kind once every enemy tower is dead. Super and mega creeps have identical stats in the dump (700 hp melee, 475 hp ranged), so mega differs only by the rule that spawns it.

Unit scale: 1 reference unit = 15.625 milli; speed is milli per tick; weapon period = AttackRate x 100 / BaseAttackSpeed x 16 ticks; health_regen is milli-hp per tick (reference hp/s x 62.5). Stats are read from `npc_units.txt` @282fad29 (health, armor, damage midpoint, rate, range, speed, vision, regen, gold midpoint, xp).

Creeps (cited): melee 550 hp, ranged 300 hp, siege 935 hp, plus super and mega variants (`creep_super`, `creep_mega`, ...). Still different from reference: the time-based creep stat and bounty growth (every 7:30; the growth numbers are not in the dump and Liquipedia and Fandom are blocked), extra melee and ranged creeps late in the game, the flagbearer, magic resistance (siege 80, flagbearer 40), which barracks upgrades the siege creep (melee is assumed, UNVERIFIED), and the lane creeps' aggro rules beyond `aggro.rs`. Hull radii are the dump's RingRadius scaled to milli.

## Towers and bases

Units defined from the dump: `tower` (T1: 1800 hp, armor 12, damage 90, range 700 reference units), `tower_t2` and `tower_t3` (2500 hp, armor 16, damage 172), `tower_t4` (2600 hp, armor 21), `barracks_melee` (2200 hp, armor 15, regen 5), `barracks_ranged` (1300 hp, armor 9), `base` (the ancient: 4500 hp, armor 23, regen 12) and `outpost` (450 hp). Tower gold and barracks gold are paid by code in reference and are not in the dump: not modelled. The map places them at the dump's real positions: the tier is in the unit name (`tower` is tier 1), two `tower_t4` stand beside each ancient, and the barracks pairs sit at their lane ends.

The base carries the `building` filter. When the last living building of a side dies, the rival wins (`win.lua`, `on_check_win`).

## Heroes

Roster: every enabled reference hero (127, from `npc_heroes` and `heroes/hero_*.txt` @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b) is a unit in `scenarios/moba/units_heroes.osd`, generated by `node scenarios/moba/tools/gen-heroes.mjs`, which also writes the hero rule block of `scenarios/moba/attr_rules.txt` (primary attribute, base attributes and per-level gains, mana, mana and health regeneration, armor, magic resistance, base attack time, damage midpoint). Sixteen of them hold their reference abilities (axe, crystal_maiden, sven, juggernaut, zuus, sniper, lion, omniknight, phantom_lancer, dazzle, lina, lich, tidehunter, slardar, antimage, vengefulspirit), generated from reference hero files by `node scenarios/moba/tools/gen-abilities.mjs` into `abilities_ruleset.osd` (what is and is not modelled: [ABILITIES.md](ABILITIES.md)); the rest have none yet (`abilities=[]`). The ten placeholder heroes (`hero`, `ironhide`, `swiftbow`, `stormcaller`, `bulwark`, `shadeblade`, `emberlord`, `gale_archer`, `frostmage`, `tidecaller`, `thornwarden`) keep their invented kits but carry the reference stats of a stand-in hero (`STANDINS` in the script: `dragon_knight`, `sven`, `drow_ranger`, `zuus`, `axe`, `riki`, `lina`, `windrunner`, `crystal_maiden`, `kunkka`, `treant`) until a real kit replaces each one.

Matched to reference (sourced): health 120 base, movement speed, attack range, vision (daytime), base armor, damage min/max midpoint, base attack time (attack period = BAT x 16 ticks / (1 + agility/100)), base attributes and gains, primary attribute, health regeneration, mana 75 base and mana regeneration. Attribute coefficients are engine constants absent from the dump and come from the reference ruleset wiki Attributes page: 22 health and 0.1 health regeneration per strength, 1/6 armor and 1 attack speed per agility, 12 mana and 0.05 mana regeneration per intelligence, 1 damage per primary point and 0.7 per point of every attribute for a universal hero. Hero level 1 is zero levels gained; level-up recomputes the stats.

Still UNVERIFIED or not modelled for heroes: hull radius 375 milli (24 reference units, an engine constant not in the dump), the night vision range, turn rate, attack animation point and projectile speed of the basic attack, magic resistance (stored, `hero_magic_resist_pct`, not yet applied by the damage code), the facets and the talent tree, the `deny_pct` of the ten placeholder heroes (25, not the reference rule that a hero cannot be denied), and the reference xp curve past what `xp.rs` carries.

Skills: a hero spends one skill point per character level. Ordinary abilities learn to rank 4 at levels 1, 3, 5 and 7 (the gate is data); the ultimate learns to rank 3 at levels 6, 12 and 18; talents come at levels 10, 15, 20 and 25, one of two per tier. The xp curve reaches level 18 (cumulative xp 100, 250, 500, ... 15200; UNVERIFIED). Mechanics covered by the roster: nuke, projectile, area damage, blink, heal over time, aura, passive stat, summon, channel, and the status effects stun, slow, silence, root and invisibility. Passives are stat abilities with cooldown 100000 cast once at spawn by a `spawned` behaviour.

## Respawn and buyback

Sources: patch notes from the reference wiki datafeed (7.24 to 7.40) and `npc_heroes`/`npc_units` @282fad29. A dead hero respawns after `12 + 3 * (level - 1)` seconds: levels 1 to 5 (12, 15, 18, 21, 24) are from the 7.24 notes, the continuation and the 100 s cap are UNVERIFIED. Buyback costs `(200 + net worth / 13) * 1.15` (7.29 and 7.36 notes; net worth is gold plus item costs); its 480 s cooldown is UNVERIFIED. Hero last-hit gold is `125 + 8 * level` (7.31 notes) and each assisting hero gets `15 + (50 + 0.037 * victim net worth) / assisting heroes` (7.40 notes). Armor reduces physical damage by `0.06a / (1 + 0.06|a|)` with no floor; heroes take ability damage reduced by their magic resistance (25 base, `hero_base`). Movement speed is held to 100..550 (7.38 notes; one reference speed point is 64 in Q16.16). The xp table (230, 600, 1080 ... 45595 for levels 2 to 30) is an engine constant recalled, UNVERIFIED past level 8. Still not reference: turn rate, attack point and backswing, projectile speed, evasion, per-unit night vision, fountain regeneration, death gold loss, streak bounties, tower and Roshan magic resistance.

## Items and shops

Source: `items.txt` and `neutral_items.txt` of SteamDatabase tracking repo @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b, turned into `scenarios/moba/items.osd` by `scenarios/moba/tools/gen-items.mjs` (do not edit the `.osd` by hand). 313 items: every non-obsolete item of the file, with reference cost (the full price), the first requirement set of its recipe as components (up to six; the recipe scroll is not an item, its price is the cost less the components), consumable and stack data (`ItemInitialCharges`, `ItemStackable`), shop stock (`ItemStockMax`, `ItemStockInitial`, `ItemStockTime`, `ItemInitialStockTime`) and the neutral tiers, with the neutral items and enhancement levels of `neutral_items.txt`. Every stat key the generator knows (`bonus_*` and the unprefixed forms of the neutral items) becomes an item stat; scale is 15.625 milli per reference unit and 16 ticks per second.

## Neutrals

Thirty-eight reference neutral types are defined as `neutral_<name>` units with dump stats (health, regen, armor, damage, attack time, range, speed, vision, bounty). Camp compositions are UNVERIFIED: small = kobold taskmaster, tunneler, kobold; medium = alpha wolf and two giant wolves; large = centaur khan and two outrunners; ancient = granite golem and two rock golems. Neutral abilities are not modelled. The map holds 28 camps of three creeps (player -1) at the dump's spawner points, 14 per side, answering attacks through the shared `attacked` behaviour. A camp unit that strays more than 10938 milli (700 reference units, UNVERIFIED) from home walks back.

The first regular camp set appears at 1:00 (`camp_first_spawn_s=60` in `attr_rules.txt`; reference's Dueling Fates update); Roshan and the bounty runes are present from the start. Camps then refill on every whole minute of the game clock, and only when no living unit stands within 4688 milli (300 reference units, UNVERIFIED) of a camp unit's home (neutral camps spawn every minute if the camp is empty, reference wiki; the camp box size is not in the dump). A camp with a survivor is not refilled, so pulling a creep away from its camp and waiting for the minute mark stacks creeps, as in reference.

## Roshan

A large neutral boss at the first pit of the dump (2831, -2740 reference units; cell 207, 206). Health 6000, damage 75 (rate 2.0 s), armor 30, regen 20, speed 270, bounty 245 gold (midpoint of a cited 200-290 range) and 400 xp (cited). Respawns a random 480 to 660 s (8 to 11 minutes, reference wiki) after death. The killer receives the `aegis` item (cost 300, UNVERIFIED). Magic resistance 55 and his abilities are not modelled. He starts in the bottom pit and from 15:00 walks to the pit of the day/night phase (top at night, bottom at day, patch 7.38; both pits from the dump's `roshan_spawner`, placed as `roshan_pit_*` markers in `waves.osd`), and he respawns in the pit of the phase he dies in. reference's drops change with each death (Aegis, then cheese or Roshan's Banner, then Refresher Shard or Aghanim's Blessing, patches 7.35 to 7.38); here only the Aegis exists.

## Runes

Two bounty runes at the dump's bounty spawners (-996, 4431) and (595, -4660). A living hero within 1500 takes 50 gold and the rune dies; it returns on the next 3 minute mark of the game clock (reference wiki). The pickup range and gold are UNVERIFIED (the reference value scales with game time). Not modelled: power runes (first at 6:00, then every 2 minutes), water runes (2:00 and 4:00), the initial set of four bounty runes at 0:00 (reference patch 7.29), shrines of wisdom (every 7 minutes, patch 7.38), lotus pools (a lotus every 3 minutes up to 6, patch 7.33), outpost capture.

## Tormentor

The Tormentor (`tormentor`, from `miniboss`: 1 health, armor 20, magic resistance 55, regeneration 20, no attack, no movement) first spawns at 15:00 and returns 10 minutes after it dies (reference patches 7.33 and 7.38). Its 3000 damage barrier is the unit shield (the dump's `miniboss_unyielding_shield` damage_absorb) regenerating 1 per tick. It spawns at the Radiant corner when the phase is night and at the Dire corner by day (patch 7.38; markers `tormentor_spawn_*`). reference pays 175 gold to every team member (7.38b) and scales the barrier, reflection (`miniboss_reflect`, 30 percent) and radiance aura (`miniboss_radiance`) with game time; here it pays 175 gold once to the killer's side and has no reflection, aura, alleviation aura or barrier growth.

## Denying

A unit may deny an allied unit (never itself) whose health is at or below its deny threshold, a percentage of max health set by `deny_pct`: hero 25, creep 50, tender 50, objective 50, tower 10, base 0. A denied unit pays no gold. A denied lane creep gives enemy heroes 50 percent of its xp; a denied hero gives 0 percent. The 50 and 0 percent xp rules and the no-gold rule are verified; every threshold is UNVERIFIED.

## Economy

Gold comes from kills. `kill_gold.lua` pays a unit's bounty when it dies (`core/bounty.lua` makes the payout): gold comes from heroes' kills only, 1:1 with Dota: a killing hero keeps the bounty less a 20 percent team share (`TEAM_SHARE_PCT`), and the killer's side gets the share; a creep, tower or neutral killer pays nothing for a unit that is not a hero; an attackerless death pays nothing; a self kill and a denial pay nothing. A hero killed by an enemy creep or tower pays its kill gold to the enemy heroes (`heroes.lua`, `creep_kill_gold`), as Dota does (the Fandom Gold page, Hero kills): the enemy heroes that damaged it within `kill_window_s` (20 seconds) share it, one damager taking all of it and several splitting it equally; with no enemy hero among its damagers, the living enemy heroes split it (dead enemy heroes are left out: UNVERIFIED against Dota). A hero killed by a neutral camp creep pays nothing, and so does a hero killed by its own team's creep. A hero that dies holding the aegis (`aegis.lua`) is paid nothing for that death. Rune pickups add gold as described under Runes. The tender is a data unit with `resource_a`; no moba rule makes it harvest.
