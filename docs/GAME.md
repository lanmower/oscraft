# How a match plays

The `scenarios/moba` scenario is a data-only match: the engine has no scenario-specific code, and every number here comes from the `.osd` files in that directory. The full tables are in [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md), generated from the same files.

**Provenance.** A figure is UNVERIFIED unless its `.osd` comment cites a source. Cited figures: tower health, armor and damage; creep health, armor, damage, gold and xp bounty; Roshan health, damage, armor, gold and xp; the cost of the blink item. The abilities of eight heroes (axe, crystal_maiden, sven, juggernaut, zuus, sniper, lion, omniknight) are Valve's, see [ABILITIES.md](ABILITIES.md). Everything else (speeds, radii, ranges, sight, item costs and stats, timers, positions, the abilities of the other heroes) is a placeholder. Where this page marks a figure UNVERIFIED, expect it to change.

Time: the sim runs 16 ticks per second. Distances are milli-units; one map cell is 1000 (64 Dota units).

## Map

The map is the Dota 2 map (patch 7.41 data) at its real GridNav size, generated by `scripts/gen-dota-map.mjs` into `map.osd` and `neutral.osd`. Do not hand-edit those two files; edit the script and rerun it (`node scripts/gen-dota-map.mjs`, which downloads the sources).

**Size.** The world spans x and y from -10464 to 10400 Dota units (20864 wide). GridNav cells are 64 units, so the grid is 327 x 327 = 106,929 cells (`grid(327, 327, cell_milli=1000)`). The source marks 47,323 cells untraversable; the generator also blocks 2,303 cells for the 2,475 trees (trees are static here, there is no tree cutting) and re-opens the footprints of buildings (units, not terrain, are the obstacles). That leaves 57,303 walkable cells, 57,215 of them connected to the Radiant fountain (the other 88 are pockets). **Scale:** 1 Dota unit = 15.625 milli, so one 64-unit cell is one 1000-milli sim cell and Dota speeds, ranges and radii keep their ratio to the map. Sim x grows east and sim y grows south on screen (sim y = -Dota y), so Radiant (team 0) is bottom-left and Dire (team 1) top-right, as in the game.

**Layout (from the dump).** Fountains at (-7456, -6938) Radiant and (7408, 6848) Dire; ancients (`base`) at (-5920, -5352) and (5528, 5000). Per side: tier 1, 2 and 3 towers on each of the top, middle and bottom lanes and two tier 4 towers beside the ancient (11 per side, 22 in all); three melee and three ranged barracks, one pair per lane; 7 filler buildings as doodads only. Four shops (one at each fountain, two on the sides at (-5080, 1948) and (4886, -1208)), two outposts at (-4096, -448) and (3392, -448), two power rune spots at (-1640, 1112) and (1180, -1216), two bounty rune spots at (-996, 4431) and (595, -4660), two Roshan pits at (2831, -2740) and (-3194, 2395), two Tormentor spawns, two lotus pools, two twin gates, two xp fountains, four landmarks and ten lanterns (doodads only). 28 neutral camps (14 per side) at the dump's spawner positions. Terrain height comes from the dump's elevation grid: level 0 river and low ground, 1 field and lanes, 2 base high ground, 3 and 4 cliffs and rims (clamped), `height_step(2000)`. The diagonal river, the high-ground ramps and the lane shapes are the data's own, not drawn by hand.

**Not sourced or approximated (UNVERIFIED).** Liquipedia and Fandom are Cloudflare-blocked and the Valve developer wiki holds no coordinates, so the GitHub dump is the only map source. Approximated: the 128-unit height step (2000 milli per level) and the clamp at level 4; the ten hero start cells (the dump has no start entity: five open cells on a 520-unit ring around each fountain, facing the ancient) and the tender and courier cells beside them; the mapping of the dump's neutralType 0..3 to small, medium, large and ancient camps (the counts 6, 14, 6 and 2 fit, nothing confirms it) and every camp composition; camp units spread on a 110-unit ring around the spawner point; trees as permanent blocks. Not modelled: Roshan sits at the first pit only (the second pit and the day/night swap are a doodad marker); power runes, Tormentors, lotus pools, twin gates and xp fountains are markers without gameplay; outposts are neutral structures without capture; the secret shops of older patches are absent from the dump.

**Sources.** `github.com/leamare/dota-map-coordinates` default branch, read on 2026-10-18: `data/worlddata.json`, `data/gridnavdata.json`, `data/elevationdata.json`, `data/mapdata.json`, `mapdata.json`, `data/lanedata.json` (a dump of the game's entities and GridNav). The Valve developer wiki page `Dota_2_Workshop_Tools/Level_Design/Dota/Navigation_Mesh` for the 64-unit GridNav cell. `SteamDatabase/GameTracking-Dota2` @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b for unit stats (see the sections below).

## Sides and players

Ten players, two teams of five: even players on team 0, odd on team 1 (`start(...)`). Each player starts with one hero and one tender at their base. The lobby pool holds the ten hero types, five per side.

## Lanes and creeps

Each base carries the `creep_wave` ability. It fires when the base spawns and again each time its 600-tick (37.5 s) cooldown ends. It spawns one creep per lane (top, middle, bottom). Each creep marches along its lane to the nearest enemy building and fights what it meets. Idle units with a weapon (creeps, towers, bases) strike the nearest enemy in reach.

The wave now fires every 480 ticks (30 s) and spawns three melee creeps and one ranged creep per lane.

Unit scale: 1 Dota unit = 15.625 milli; speed is milli per tick; weapon period = AttackRate x 100 / BaseAttackSpeed x 16 ticks; health_regen is milli-hp per tick (Dota hp/s x 62.5). Stats below are read from `npc_units.txt` @282fad29 (health, armor, damage midpoint, rate, range, speed, vision, regen, gold midpoint, xp).

Creeps (cited): melee 550 hp, ranged 300 hp, siege 935 hp, plus super and mega variants (`creep_super`, `creep_mega`, ...). The super and mega variants exist as units but nothing spawns them yet: barracks-loss upgrades and the time-based wave stat growth are not modelled. Siege creeps are not in the wave yet (every fifth wave in Dota). Magic resistance (siege 80, flagbearer 40) is not modelled. Hull radii are recalled, not read from the dump (UNVERIFIED).

## Towers and bases

Units defined from the dump: `tower` (T1: 1800 hp, armor 12, damage 90, range 700 Dota units), `tower_t2` and `tower_t3` (2500 hp, armor 16, damage 172), `tower_t4` (2600 hp, armor 21), `barracks_melee` (2200 hp, armor 15, regen 5), `barracks_ranged` (1300 hp, armor 9), `base` (the ancient: 4500 hp, armor 23, regen 12) and `outpost` (450 hp). Tower gold and barracks gold are paid by code in Dota and are not in the dump: not modelled. The map places them at the dump's real positions: the tier is in the unit name (`tower` is tier 1), two `tower_t4` stand beside each ancient, and the barracks pairs sit at their lane ends.

The base carries the `building` filter. When the last living building of a side dies, the rival wins (`win_on_base_loss` in `behaviours.osd`).

## Heroes

Roster: every enabled Dota hero (127, from `npc_heroes` and `heroes/npc_dota_hero_*.txt` @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b) is a unit in `scenarios/moba/units_heroes.osd`, generated by `node scripts/gen-heroes.mjs`, which also writes the `hero <unit> ...` lines of `scenarios/moba/hero_rules.txt` (primary attribute, base attributes and per-level gains, mana, mana and health regeneration, armor, magic resistance, base attack time, damage midpoint). Eight of them (axe, crystal_maiden, sven, juggernaut, zuus, sniper, lion, omniknight) hold their Dota abilities, generated from Valve's hero files by `node scripts/gen-abilities.mjs` into `abilities_dota.osd` (what is and is not modelled: [ABILITIES.md](ABILITIES.md)); the rest have none yet (`abilities=[]`). The ten placeholder heroes (`hero`, `ironhide`, `swiftbow`, `stormcaller`, `bulwark`, `shadeblade`, `emberlord`, `gale_archer`, `frostmage`, `tidecaller`, `thornwarden`) keep their invented kits but carry the Valve stats of a stand-in hero (`STANDINS` in the script: `dragon_knight`, `sven`, `drow_ranger`, `zuus`, `axe`, `riki`, `lina`, `windrunner`, `crystal_maiden`, `kunkka`, `treant`) until a real kit replaces each one.

Matched to Valve (sourced): health 120 base, movement speed, attack range, vision (daytime), base armor, damage min/max midpoint, base attack time (attack period = BAT x 16 ticks / (1 + agility/100)), base attributes and gains, primary attribute, health regeneration, mana 75 base and mana regeneration. Attribute coefficients are engine constants absent from the dump and come from the Dota 2 wiki Attributes page: 22 health and 0.1 health regeneration per strength, 1/6 armor and 1 attack speed per agility, 12 mana and 0.05 mana regeneration per intelligence, 1 damage per primary point and 0.7 per point of every attribute for a universal hero. Hero level 1 is zero levels gained; level-up recomputes the stats.

Still UNVERIFIED or not modelled for heroes: hull radius 375 milli (24 Dota units, an engine constant not in the dump), the night vision range, turn rate, attack animation point and projectile speed of the basic attack, magic resistance (stored, `hero_magic_resist_pct`, not yet applied by the damage code), the facets and the talent tree, the `deny_pct` of the ten placeholder heroes (25, not the Dota rule that a hero cannot be denied), and the Dota xp curve past what `xp.rs` carries.

Skills: a hero spends one skill point per character level. Ordinary abilities learn to rank 4 at levels 1, 3, 5 and 7 (the gate is data); the ultimate learns to rank 3 at levels 6, 12 and 18; talents come at levels 10, 15, 20 and 25, one of two per tier. The xp curve reaches level 18 (cumulative xp 100, 250, 500, ... 15200; UNVERIFIED). Mechanics covered by the roster: nuke, projectile, area damage, blink, heal over time, aura, passive stat, summon, channel, and the status effects stun, slow, silence, root and invisibility. Passives are stat abilities with cooldown 100000 cast once at spawn by a `spawned` behaviour.

## Respawn and buyback

Sources: patch notes from the dota2.com datafeed (7.24 to 7.40) and `npc_heroes`/`npc_units` @282fad29. A dead hero respawns after `12 + 3 * (level - 1)` seconds: levels 1 to 5 (12, 15, 18, 21, 24) are from the 7.24 notes, the continuation and the 100 s cap are UNVERIFIED. Buyback costs `(200 + net worth / 13) * 1.15` (7.29 and 7.36 notes; net worth is gold plus item costs); its 480 s cooldown is UNVERIFIED. Hero last-hit gold is `125 + 8 * level` (7.31 notes) and each assisting hero gets `15 + (50 + 0.037 * victim net worth) / assisting heroes` (7.40 notes). Armor reduces physical damage by `0.06a / (1 + 0.06|a|)` with no floor; heroes take ability damage reduced by their magic resistance (25 base, `npc_dota_hero_base`). Movement speed is held to 100..550 (7.38 notes; one Dota speed point is 64 in Q16.16). The xp table (230, 600, 1080 ... 45595 for levels 2 to 30) is an engine constant recalled, UNVERIFIED past level 8. Still not Dota: turn rate, attack point and backswing, projectile speed, evasion, per-unit night vision, fountain regeneration, death gold loss, streak bounties, tower and Roshan magic resistance.

## Items and shops

Source: `items.txt` and `neutral_items.txt` of SteamDatabase/GameTracking-Dota2 @282fad29f0f74504fad465c52d197fb61f4d612b, turned into `scenarios/moba/items.osd` by `scripts/gen-items.mjs` (do not edit the `.osd` by hand). 313 items: every non-obsolete item of the file, with Valve's cost (the full price), the first requirement set of its recipe as components (up to six; the recipe scroll is not an item, its price is the cost less the components), consumable and stack data (`ItemInitialCharges`, `ItemStackable`), shop stock (`ItemStockMax`, `ItemStockInitial`, `ItemStockTime`, `ItemInitialStockTime`) and the neutral tiers, with the neutral items and enhancement levels of `neutral_items.txt`. Every stat key the generator knows (`bonus_*` and the unprefixed forms of the neutral items) becomes an item stat; scale is 15.625 milli per Dota unit and 16 ticks per second.

A hero has six main slots, three backpack slots, one neutral slot and a nine-slot stash (UNVERIFIED layout, recalled). Main and neutral slots are active: stats and abilities apply. A neutral item fits only the neutral slot and the backpack. Buying needs gold, a shop in reach that stocks the item (4000 milli, UNVERIFIED), team stock when the item has a stock limit, and a free slot (overflow goes to the stash). Buying an item consumes the components held in the inventory or stash (the parts of a missing component too) and charges the cost less theirs, as the game's auto-combine does. Stackable items join stacks. Selling returns half the cost (UNVERIFIED: the full refund of the first moments is not modelled). Items stay with a hero through death.

Shops: home shop (doodad `shop`) sells every item that is not a secret-shop item; the secret shop (doodad `secret_shop`) sells the 17 secret items; side shops (doodad `side_shop`) sell basic components and consumables. The side shop list is a heuristic (`shops.txt` is not in the dump), UNVERIFIED. The map lane places the side and secret shop doodads.

Modelled item effects: all stats that map to engine stats (armor, damage, health, mana, strength, agility, intelligence, movement and attack speed, regeneration, magic resistance, evasion, lifesteal, spell lifesteal, spell amplification, cooldown reduction, status resistance, crit, cleave, bash, percent movement speed, attack range), the actives of 59 items (blink family, healing salve, faerie fire, clarity, mango, cheese, wards, town portal scroll and travel boots as a 3 s channelled teleport to a friendly unit, black king bar, hex, silence, stun, disarm, mute, root, dagon 1-5, ethereal blade, shiva's guard, meteor hammer, cyclone, refresher, mekansm, guardian greaves, arcane boots, pipe, satanic, mask of madness, manta, ghost scepter, shadow blade and silver edge, drums, dust, smoke of deceit, blood grenade and more), the passives of maelstrom, mjollnir, desolator, skadi, orb of venom, monkey king bar, radiance, vladmir's offering, headdress, ring of basilius and buckler, the Aegis (reincarnates in 5 s where the hero fell, without a death booked), Aghanim's Scepter (held), Aghanim's Shard and Blessing (spent).

Still different from Dota 2, by reason: neutral item passives and actives other than their stats and enhancement effects (not yet translated from `AbilityValues`); a neutral item drop flow (tier start times and craft costs are in `neutral_items.txt`, not played yet); bottle, tango, magic stick and wand, urn, spirit vessel, hand of midas, armlet and power treads toggles (charges and toggles need engine mechanics not written); force staff and hurricane pike push (no displacement); blade mail, crimson guard, lotus orb, linken's sphere, bloodstone, aeon disk (conditional passives); dominator helms (no dominate); courier items; Aegis and Roshan items expiring; cast ranges apply to actives but several targeting rules are simplified; stats of items whose keys are conditional (melee against ranged values use the melee value); fractional multipliers (evasion and magic resistance add up instead of multiplying).

## Neutrals

Thirty-eight Valve neutral types are defined as `neutral_<name>` units with dump stats (health, regen, armor, damage, attack time, range, speed, vision, bounty). Camp compositions are UNVERIFIED: small = kobold taskmaster, tunneler, kobold; medium = alpha wolf and two giant wolves; large = centaur khan and two outrunners; ancient = granite golem and two rock golems. Neutral abilities are not modelled. The map holds 28 camps of three creeps (player -1) at the dump's spawner points, 14 per side, answering attacks through the shared `attacked` behaviour. A camp unit that strays more than 6000 milli from home walks back; a dead one respawns at home after 60 s (UNVERIFIED).

## Roshan

A large neutral boss at the first pit of the dump (2831, -2740 Dota units; cell 207, 206). Health 6000, damage 75 (rate 2.0 s), armor 30, regen 20, speed 270, bounty 245 gold (midpoint of a cited 200-290 range) and 400 xp (cited). Respawns 480 s after death (UNVERIFIED). The killer receives the `aegis` item (cost 300, UNVERIFIED).

## Runes

Two bounty runes at the dump's bounty spawners (-996, 4431) and (595, -4660). A living hero within 1500 takes 50 gold and the rune dies; it respawns after 180 s. The pickup range, gold and respawn numbers are UNVERIFIED.

## Denying

A unit may deny an allied unit (never itself) whose health is at or below its deny threshold, a percentage of max health set by `deny_pct`: hero 25, creep 50, tender 50, objective 50, tower 10, base 0. A denied unit pays no gold. A denied lane creep gives enemy heroes 50 percent of its xp; a denied hero gives 0 percent. The 50 and 0 percent xp rules and the no-gold rule are verified; every threshold is UNVERIFIED.

## Economy

Tenders harvest `resource_a`, which is the gold of this match. Creep and Roshan kills pay their bounty to the killer's side.
