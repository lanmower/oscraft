# 24. Power tier draft (UNVERIFIED)

**Status: tier letters withdrawn.** The first pass put 64 of 112 heroes in A, so the letters measure nothing. The flag list and the test-first list stay: they are what the first playtest should check. UNVERIFIED throughout.

UNVERIFIED. No simulation exists. Flags are read from the kit text (sections 9, 10, 21). This is not a power budget or a balance result.

## Method

- Timed disables scale with the ability 2 table, so only disables with no timer or an until-condition count as long.
- Stacks with a stated cap count as stacking but are not flagged. Talent choices count only when they add a global, uncapped-stacking or no-timer disable effect.
- FLAG: global effect, permanent disable (no timer or until-condition), or stacking with no stated cap. Flagged heroes are tested first.

## 3. Test first

1. Who Asked (036): global ult plus fade escape. Measure whether delayed global damage lands and how often it can be dodged.
2. Pit Boss (112): Atrophy Feed grows in combat with no cap stated. Measure aura damage over long fights and gate teleport use.
3. Spawn Farm (013): spiderlings with no cap stated. Measure spiderlings alive over a match and heal from spider kills.
4. Lore Keeper (029): map-wide fissure. Measure how often it hits heroes outside the caster's view and its share of kills.
5. Multi-Accounter (066): more illusions with no cap stated, plus a blink. Measure illusions alive over time and their total damage.
6. Teddy Bear Mode (100): Claw Swipes stacks with no cap stated. Measure stacks at fight end and damage per stack.
7. Lumberjack Take (093): Stack Armor with no cap stated. Measure armor at fight end against damage taken.
8. Hot Take (028): talent makes Echo Chamber global. Measure win rate of the global choice against the local one.
9. Ban Hammer (088): talent grows Escalate per cast with no cap stated. Measure the damage multiplier after repeated casts in one fight.
10. Looping Clip (068): ult stun has no timer. Measure the longest stun and whether target death or clip end ends it.
