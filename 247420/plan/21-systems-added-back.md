### Backpack

Three backpack slots, indexed 7 to 9. Backpack items give their passive stats. Active items do not work from the backpack. Sell-back rules match the inventory.

### Neutral items

- One neutral item slot per hero. It sits outside the six inventory slots and the backpack.
- Neutral items drop from jungle camps. A hero gets one choice of three. A new neutral replaces the old one.
- Tier timings: tier 1 from 7:00, tier 2 from 17:00, tier 3 from 27:00, tier 4 from 37:00.

| Tier | Source | Items (choose one of three) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Small camps | Thread Pin: +10 damage. Crumb Pouch: +1.5 HP regen. NPC Badge: +8% spell amplification. |
| 2 | Medium camps | Echo Ring: 8% attack lifesteal. Stan Sign: allied creeps in Reach gain +5% damage. Hot Mic: 15% chance on attack to silence for 0.6 s. |
| 3 | Hard camps | Viral Bell: 10% chance on attack to heal 60 HP. Ratio Cloak: 8% damage reduction above 50 Clout. Cancel Mask: +6% magic resistance, +50 max mana. |
| 4 | Old Forum Thread | Eternal Thread: +150 HP, +20 damage, 10% faster cooldowns. Vintage Post: active, 2 s reveal and 1.5 s stun in 400 radius, 60 s cooldown. Long Tail: +25% Clout gain. |

### Safe Spots

- Two Safe Spots per team, one at each river crossing next to the team's safe lane. Available from 0:00.
- Active: heals all allies within 500 radius for 14% of max HP per second, for 5 s.
- Cooldown 45 s, shared per Safe Spot.

### Lite Plan

- Cost 1,400 gold. A standalone item, not built from Pro Plan.
- Adds one second effect to a basic ability on the holder. The effect is set per hero in section 9.
- Stacks with Pro Plan.

### Decoy Posts

- Fifteen Decoy Post buildings per base. Each has 500 HP, no armor and a 5 gold bounty.
- Decoy Posts do not count toward tiers or the win condition. Creeps do not target them.

### Stat Boost

- A fifth skill. It takes unspent ability points, after the three basic skills and the ultimate are maxed.
- Each point gives +2 to all three attributes. Maximum 10 points.

### Chat Check

- Reveals enemy heroes within 900 radius for 8 s.
- Shared team cooldown of 270 s. Any hero on the team can cast it.

### Status resistance

- Base 0%. Items and talents add it.
- Reduces the duration of stuns, silences, roots, polymorphs, fear and taunts. Cap 50%.

### Algorithm Boost

- Spawns with the other boosts on the river. Lasts 50 s.
- While held: Reach +100 and a violet pip on the minimap. No cooldown or mana change.

### Gamer Fuel

- The Trend drops Gamer Fuel on its second kill and every kill after.
- Gamer Fuel cannot be sold. Chug it to restore 2,500 HP and 1,000 mana over 4 s. One can per drop.

### Pit Boss (roster entry 112)

- **Name:** Pit Boss. Archetype: Moderator. Attribute: Strength. Range: Melee.
- **Gag:** Runs the pit. Has never been to it.
- **Ability 1, Pit Post:** area trap in 400 radius that roots and damages.
- **Ability 2, Atrophy Feed:** aura that damages nearby enemies, growing by 10% per second in combat.
- **Ability 3, Gate Crash:** places a gate at a point. Allies who step through teleport to the other gate.
- **Ultimate, Hard Fork:** teleports an ally to a point, then damages enemies around the arrival.

### Seasons

A season is one patch. Each season rotates three things and changes no numbers:

- The Trend modifier pool (section 12).
- The phrase pool for the announcer and chat wheel (sections 16 and 18).
- The cosmetic skin theme: costumes, plinths and the Trend banner.

Numbers change only in the balance pass.

### Spectator reactions

- Spectators send reactions from the chat wheel (section 18). Each viewer has a 10 s cooldown per reaction.
- Reactions show to spectators only. They have no effect on play.
- Ranked matches show no spectator reactions on the player screen.
