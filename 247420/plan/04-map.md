# 4. Map
The map is two halves split by a river. Each half holds three lanes, a jungle and a base.

### Bases

- **Morning Feed** (ally): light value, sunrise palette. The river is the Feed, with slow caustics.
- **Late Night Feed** (enemy): dark value, screen glow.
- Each base holds the Spawn Lounge, a main shop at the Spawn Lounge, one Back Alley Shop, two Side Shops (one each on the top and bottom lanes), and a Trending Pit.

### Lanes

Three lanes per team: top, mid and bottom. Lane assignment by role is in section 14. Side Shops sit at the lane mouths and sell basic components.

### River

- The river splits the map. It holds the two boost spots and the Trend pit.
- Two boost spots on the river. Boosts spawn every 2 min (section 12).
- Shallow crossings let heroes cross the river at fixed points only.

### Jungle

Each team has a primary and a secondary jungle.

| Jungle | Camps | Stack rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | 2 hard, 2 medium, 1 small | Pulled camps respawn on the minute |
| Secondary | 1 hard, 1 elder | Elders are magic-resistant, not spell-immune (see section 12) |
| Near Back Alley Shop | 1 hard | Guards the Back Alley Shop approach |

Jungle creature names are in section 12.

### Structure list

Towers, Content Mills, Original Post and Spawn Lounge are in section 3. Shops, inventory and items are in section 11. Wards are in section 13.

### Map props and marginalia

Out-of-bounds marginalia (ten static vignettes) sit on the map edge. They never block movement and never hide a unit. Full list in section 18.
