# 14. Draft and modes
The draft is the first decision of the match. It sets the hero pool, the lane plan and the item plan.

### Game modes

| Mode | Use | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Showrunner Draft | Ranked and main | Two captains ban and pick in turn. 30 s per pick, 130 s bonus time per team. |
| Open Feed | Casual | Each player picks freely. No bans. |
| Algorithm Draft | Casual | Heroes drawn from a 50-hero pool. |
| Shuffle Draft | Casual | Each player picks from a random 10-hero set. |
| Ranked Open Feed | Ranked | Each player nominates a hero, then half the nominated heroes are banned at random. |
| Short Form | Casual or ranked, 25 minute target | Two lanes per side, mid removed. Trend opens at 5:00. Original Post falls once both T2s on a lane are down. Surrender vote at 15 min. Details in the Short Form section below. UNVERIFIED |

### Showrunner Draft order

Ten bans and ten picks, alternating by team. The order (final order):

1. Ban: 1st, 2nd, 1st, 2nd
2. Pick: 1st, 2nd, 2nd, 1st
3. Ban: 2nd, 1st, 2nd, 1st
4. Pick: 2nd, 1st, 2nd, 1st
5. Ban: 2nd, 1st
6. Pick: 1st, 2nd

### Short Form

Short Form is the same game on a smaller map. All numbers are UNVERIFIED.

- Two lanes per side (top and bottom). Mid lane and its tower and Content Mill rows are removed.
- The Trend opens at 5:00 and respawns in 4 to 6 min.
- Original Post is invulnerable until both T2s on a lane fall, not both T4s.
- XP per level is multiplied by 1.5 for the same level thresholds.
- Clout gain, Reach, Stan and the Repost chain work as in the full game.

### Roles

| Position | Role | Lane | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Carry | Safe lane | Clout carry or late-game damage |
| 2 | Mid | Mid | Highest-impact solo lane |
| 3 | Offlane | Off lane | Initiator, tank or disruptor |
| 4 | Roamer | Safe lane, with carry | Soft support, gank pressure |
| 5 | Hard support | Safe lane, with carry | Vision, protection, Stan support |

Jungler is a viable role for heroes with camp-clearing kits (Comment Leech, Touch-Grass Hermit, Lurker archetype). A jungler is a position choice, not a sixth slot.

### Archetype lanes

Lane preference by archetype. This is guidance for the draft UI, not a restriction.

- Carry: Algorithm-Pilled Carry, Influencer, Doomscroller
- Mid: Doomscroller, Ghost Account, Hot Take
- Offlane: Moderator, Hot Take
- Roamer: Lurker, Reply Guy, Parasocial
- Hard support: Notification Support, Archivist

### Ban phase

- Bans are hidden until the phase ends, then revealed together.
- A banned hero cannot be picked by either team for the match.
- Pick and ban rates are tracked for balance (section 19).

### Pick screen

The pick screen is titled "Draft your content plan." It shows the archetype role icon on every hero, and the Trend modifier for the match once it is known.
