# 16. Humor and tone
The register is deadpan, corporate and analytics. One image carries two readings. The second reading rewards a deliberate look.

### Rules

1. Jokes target observed behavior: posting, reposting, ratios, moderation.
2. Jokes never sit on the gameplay read path. A signal (LIVE, Stan, Go Viral, Ratio) is always readable without its joke.
3. Voice is calm and under-acted. Nothing shouts except the Hot Take archetype, and that is also a joke.
4. Gags are always on. They never sit on the read path, so nothing needs a switch.
5. Meme vocabulary is generic internet usage only (cope, ratio, based, touch grass, NPC). No brand names, named creators, franchise characters or owned catchphrases. Any phrase can rotate out per patch.
6. Loaded phrases (cope, seethe, mald, skill issue, cry about it, L + ratio, ok boomer, learn to code) are allowed in the Loaded tier. They aim at in-game play, never at a person's identity: no slurs, no group-targeting slogans, no real people or events. There is no switch: the game is loaded on purpose.
