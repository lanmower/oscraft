# 16. Humor and tone
The register is maximalist, meme-forward and then some. Two images carry two readings. The second reading is the punchline.

### Rules
1. Jokes target observed behavior: posting, reposting, ratios, moderation, engagement farming.
2. Every joke has a setup and a punchline. The setup is readable without the punchline, but the punchline earns the laugh.
3. Voice is expressive and reactive. The Hot Take archetype screams; everyone else reacts.
4. Gags have a lifespan. They expire, rotate, or get folded into the next patch.
5. Meme vocabulary embraces original game-specific humor and generic internet usage (cope, ratio, touch grass, NPC). Phrases rotate per patch to keep the comedy fresh without leaning on external viral trends.
6. Loaded phrases (cope, seethe, mald, skill issue, cry about it, L + ratio, ok boomer, learn to code) are in the Loaded tier. They aim at in-game play, never at a person's identity: no slurs, no group-targeting slogans, no real people or events.
7. Hillarity is the goal. Relevance is the constraint. A joke that lands earns its keep; a joke that's just loud is cut.
