# 12. Neutrals, boosts, The Trend and Screenshot
### Jungle camps

Camp types: small, medium, hard and elder. Elders are magic-resistant (not spell-immune).

| Creature | Camp type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NPCs | Small | Repeat one voice line. Mirrors the Morning Feed palette |
| Spam | Small | Fast, low HP |
| Shill | Medium | Casts a slowing aura |
| Troll-Bridge | Hard | Blocks a bridge crossing until killed |
| Old Forum Thread | Elder | Long-lived creature, magic-resistant |
| Hot Mic, Echo Chamber, Thread Sprite, Bio Bot, Viral Golem, Cancel Mob, Ratio Hound | Mixed | Stats by camp type, below |

Stacking:

- A camp respawns on the minute if its spawn box is empty.
- Pulling a camp out at about :53 to :55 stacks an extra camp for the next minute.
- Pulling a camp into the lane resets the lane's creep equilibrium.
- Camp stat budgets: Small 300 HP, Medium 550, Hard 950, Elder 1,600, with damage rising one step per tier (UNVERIFIED; section 19 budgets cover heroes, not neutrals).
- Stage camps: the camps beside the river's shallow crossings. A pull made while an enemy hero can see the camp gives +1 Clout per creature killed. Unwatched pulls give normal gold only.

| Boost | Effect | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sweat Mode | Double damage | 40 s |
| Dank Memes (Haste) | Movement speed boost | 25 s |
| Dank Memes (Illusion) | Spawns an illusion of self | 40 s |
| Dank Memes (Invisibility) | Invisibility until it attacks or casts | 30 s |
| Dank Memes (Regeneration) | Heal over time | 20 s |

### The Trend

A faceless boss built from stacked trending banners.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| HP | 8,000, plus 175 per minute |
| Magic resistance | 55% |
| Respawn | 8 to 11 min, random |
| Reward | Gold and XP. The killing team picks one of two rotating modifiers for 4 min. The killer takes the Screenshot. |
| Screenshot | One revive at the Spawn Lounge after a 3 s rewind clip. Expires 5 min after pickup. |

### Trend modifiers

Eight modifiers rotate each patch. Two are offered per kill:

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Challenge | +10% move speed for 3 s after each cast |
| Unboxing | −15% item cooldowns |
| Learn to Code | +10% XP from all sources, abilities cost 5% more mana |
| Speedrun | +15% move speed, towers deal +10% damage to you, and your team earns 10% less tower gold |
| Storytime | Channeled abilities finish 25% faster and cost 15% more mana |
| Get Ready With Me | Items bought at the Spawn Lounge cost 10% less. Respawn takes 2 s longer. |
| Algorithm Surge | Clout gain +20%, Clout decay +50% |
| Doomscroll | +8% ability damage while no ally is within 600, -10% move speed |

The modifier shows on the minimap timer and as a team-colored HUD filter for its duration.

### Breaking News (event)

Two events per match, at 18:00 and 30:00 (UNVERIFIED). A banner slides in using the Trend banner effect, and a 60 s capture zone opens at the river center.

- **Capture:** a team with heroes inside and no enemy hero inside holds the zone. After 20 s held, its heroes get Front Page for 40 s: Reach +100.
- **Contest:** if both teams have heroes inside, no progress is made.
- **Underdog:** a team in the Underdog mood (section 6) captures at double speed.

Front Page changes Reach only. It does not reveal anyone.

### Screenshot

The Screenshot is held by one hero. The Screenshot expires 5 min after pickup. A hero holding it at the Spawn Lounge can revive once. The revive replays the death as a rewound clip. The Screenshot is the revive reward (Participation Trophy, section 11).
